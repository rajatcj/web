Lead Consultant

Dr. Deepa Sharma

BDS MDS – Oral Medicine & Maxillofacial Radiology BPKIHS Dharan

Dr. Deepa Sharma is the founder and lead consultant at Crystal Dental and Oral Medicine Centre. With a Master of Dental Surgery specialising in Oral Medicine and Maxillofacial Radiology from B.P. Koirala Institute of Health Sciences (BPKIHS) Dharan — one of Nepal's most prestigious health institutions — she brings specialist-level expertise to every patient she treats.

Known for her gentle, patient-centred approach, Dr. Deepa takes the time to thoroughly explain diagnoses and treatment plans. Her ability to put even the most anxious patients at ease has earned her glowing reviews from patients across Nepal and internationally.

She is particularly skilled in the diagnosis and management of complex oral mucosal diseases, TMJ disorders, and maxillofacial radiology interpretation — services rarely available at general dental clinics.

  • Qualification: BDS; MDS – Oral Medicine & Maxillofacial Radiology
  • Institution: BPKIHS Dharan, Nepal
  • NMC Registration: No. 13488
  • Speciality: Oral Medicine, Maxillofacial Radiology, Oral Mucosal Diseases
  • Clinic: Crystal Dental, Nagdhunga, Pokhara
