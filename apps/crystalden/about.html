Who We Are

Pokhara's Home for Expert Oral Health

Crystal Dental and Oral Medicine Centre was established in 2021 by a team of qualified and experienced dental professionals with a shared vision: to bring specialist-level dental care to the Pokhara community in a warm, welcoming environment.

Located on the Nagdhunga Main Road opposite Charak Hospital, our clinic combines advanced diagnostic technology with genuine patient-centred care. We believe oral health is inseparable from overall wellbeing — and we treat every patient with that in mind.
