What We Offer

Our Dental Services

Comprehensive oral health care — from routine prevention to specialist oral medicine — all under one roof in Pokhara.

Preventive & Restorative

General Dentistry

The foundation of lifelong oral health — regular check-ups, cleanings, and targeted treatments to keep your smile in perfect condition.

Preventive

Dental Check-ups

Comprehensive oral examinations to detect issues early and maintain optimal dental health. Recommended every 6 months.

Preventive

Scaling & Cleaning

Professional removal of plaque and tartar build-up to prevent gum disease and keep your teeth sparkling clean.

Restorative

Fillings & Restorations

Tooth-coloured composite fillings and restorations that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, restoring function and aesthetics.

Restorative

Root Canal Treatment

Painless endodontic therapy to save infected teeth, relieve pain, and restore full function — without extraction.

Restorative

Crowns & Bridges

Custom-fabricated ceramic crowns and bridges to restore damaged teeth and replace missing ones with natural-looking results.

Paediatric

Children's Dentistry

Gentle, friendly dental care for children — building healthy habits and positive dental experiences from an early age.

Specialist Care

Oral Medicine & Maxillofacial Radiology

Our unique specialist strength — expert diagnosis and management of complex oral diseases, mucosal conditions, and jaw disorders.

Oral Medicine

Oral Lesion Diagnosis

Expert evaluation and management of oral mucosal lesions including ulcers, white patches (leukoplakia), lichen planus, and candidiasis.

Radiology

Dental Radiography

Advanced digital X-rays and maxillofacial radiological interpretation for precise diagnosis of dental and jaw conditions.

Oral Medicine

TMJ Disorders

Diagnosis and non-surgical management of temporomandibular joint disorders — relieving jaw pain, clicking, and restricted movement.

Aesthetics

Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with confidence — our cosmetic treatments are designed to complement your natural beauty.

Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

Safe, effective professional whitening treatments to remove stains and brighten your smile by several shades.

Cosmetic

Dental Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain or composite veneers to correct chips, discolouration, gaps, and misalignment — a complete smile makeover.

Cosmetic

Smile Makeovers

Comprehensive aesthetic treatment plans combining multiple procedures for a complete, harmonious, and beautiful smile transformation.

Advanced Care

Orthodontics, Implants & Surgery

Orthodontics

Braces & Aligners

Metal braces and clear aligner options to straighten teeth and correct bite issues for both teens and adults.

Implants

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacements using titanium implants — the gold standard for missing teeth.

Oral Surgery

Extractions & Minor Surgery

Gentle tooth extractions, wisdom tooth removal, and minor oral surgical procedures performed with precision and care.
