About the studio

We design quietly, and build carefully.

A small, multi-disciplinary team of architects, engineers and craftspeople in Pokhara, turning honest briefs into spaces that age with grace.

Our story

From engineering firm to full design studio.

Astak Engineers & Construction (AEC) began as a consultancy of renowned professional engineers serving Pokhara’s residential and commercial sector. As clients asked for help finishing what we had built, Astak Eng. & Constructions grew naturally, a studio that pairs structural reliability with the warmth of considered interior design.

Today our work spans private homes, boutique hospitality and workplace interiors across the Gandaki region, every project supervised on-site by qualified engineers.
