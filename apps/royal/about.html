Our Story

A studio rooted in craft and lake-city light.

Royal Interior Designer was founded in Pokhara with a simple idea: Nepali homes and businesses deserve world-class interiors, designed and delivered locally. From a small studio at BuddhaChowk, we've grown into a full-service team handling design, 3D visualization, joinery, finishes and project management under one roof.

We work across Pokhara, Kathmandu, Butwal, Lamjung and Dharan, bringing the same thoughtful, honest design process to every project, whether it's a 2-bedroom apartment or a 40-key boutique hotel.
