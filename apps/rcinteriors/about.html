Our Story & Mission

A women-led studio turning Nepal's interiors into extraordinary experiences — one space at a time.

Our Origin

Born in Pokhara, Built for Nepal

RC Interior Pvt. Ltd was founded with a simple but powerful belief: every person deserves to live and work in a space that inspires them. Based at Mustang Chowk, Pokhara, our studio began as a small but passionate team of designers committed to bringing world-class interior design to Nepal.

We started with homes in Pokhara and Kathmandu, and quickly expanded across the country — from Baglung and Kusma to Biratnagar and Bharatpur. Today, we're proud to have delivered 150+ projects and to be one of Nepal's most trusted interior design companies.

As a women-owned business, we bring a uniquely empathetic and detail-oriented lens to every project. We believe beautiful spaces change lives — and we're here to prove it, one room at a time.
